\section{Tools, hardware and setup}
\label{sec:setup}
Every image in this report is a capture made by the program, a plot of the code's own formulas or a block of the
formula-driven workbook \code{report/workbooks/cg\_workbook.xlsx}. Every count, size and coordinate comes from the
program's scene export (\code{ts --export-docs}) through a script (\code{tools/v10}, \code{tools/report}); none is
typed by hand.

\textbf{Time scale.} In automatic mode one in-game day lasts \daySeconds{}\,s (\dayMinutes{} real minutes): 10\,s per
in-game hour by day, 13.3\,s per hour by night, and 1.5 times slower while the sun is within $6^\circ$ of the horizon. The clock can also be set or scrubbed by hand.

\textbf{Determinism.} The simulation runs at a fixed 60\,Hz tick and every random stream derives from the seed. A
recorded session replays bit for bit: 60\,s recorded with random input and variable frame times, written to text and
replayed, reproduce all 60 per-second world hashes.
