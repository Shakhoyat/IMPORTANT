\section{Hard problems and trade-offs}
\label{sec:hard}
\here{6}
Six problems where the class method alone falls short in a scene of this size. Table~\ref{tab:hard} gives the
reason, the fix and what was measured; timings marked $^\ast$ were taken on a GPU shared with other jobs.

\normalfont Problem & Why it falls short & Solution & Measured\\\midrule
Z-fighting & $z_w$ is hyperbolic in distance; with $F/N=12\,000$ a 24-bit buffer resolves only $\sim$1\,m at 100\,m
  (Figure~\ref{fig:depth}b) & decals lifted 2--4\,mm above their slab; the 0.05\,m near plane stays for the
  first-person rifle & raising $N$ to 0.5\,m: $10\times$ precision, but the rifle clips\\
Light budget & Eq.~\eqref{eq:phong} loops over every light per fragment; a shadowed spot needs its own shadow map &
  1 shadowed spot (UAV-1) + 1 unshadowed spot (UAV-2) + $\le$16 practicals; searchlights off by day & bounded:
  1 key + 2 spots + $\le$16 lamps per fragment\\
Grass & one draw or one 12-triangle cube per blade: 6.3--7.7\,M triangles & instanced 5-triangle strips, culled per

Convolution & a $k\times k$ kernel costs $k^2$ multiply-adds per pixel, twice per tick & the binomial PSF is

Shadow acne & a constant bias large enough for slopes lifts contact shadows off the ground & normal offset growing with

  0.10--0.17\,ms$^\ast$\\
Transparency, exposure & rotor discs, beams and particles are translucent; entering the hall changes brightness by
  several stops & translucent parts after the opaque pass, depth writes off (unsorted); exposure adapts with two time
  constants and a +1.25\,EV indoor bias & 0.35\,s brighter, 1.1\,s darker\\
