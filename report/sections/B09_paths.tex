\section{Line follower and MicroMouse logic}
\label{sec:paths}
\here{2}
Each intruder rover combines two robot-contest machines. \textbf{Planning (MicroMouse):} on $80\times80$ cells of
0.5\,m, cells hidden from the \ab{598}{UAVs} are goals; a breadth-first flood fill spreads distances from them and the rover walks
downhill. \textbf{Driving (line follower):} the path becomes a guide line, five sensors read it, and a
\ab{599}{PD} controller sets the two wheel speeds. The soldiers use A* (1\,m cells, 0.40\,m clearance)
smoothed into centripetal Catmull--Rom splines (Figures~\ref{fig:rovermap}, \ref{fig:guardmap}).
\begin{equation}
d(c)=1+\min_{n\in N_4(c)}d(n),\ d(\text{goal})=0;\qquad
e=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{5}w_i s_i}{\sum_i s_i},\ \ u=K_p e+K_d\dot e,\ \ v_{L,R}=v\mp u
\label{eq:paths}
\end{equation}
with $w_i$ the sensors' lateral offsets, $s_i\in\{0,1\}$ their readings, $K_p=3.2$, $K_d=0.25$.

\begin{algorithm}[htbp]
\caption{Intruder rover, one tick (\code{ai/HideController.cpp}). Short forms: UAV: uncrewed aerial vehicle.}
\label{alg:intruder}
\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require UAV positions $O$, rover pose $(\mathbf{p},\psi)$, grid of \gv{kGridCells}$\times$\gv{kGridCells} cells of \gv{kCellSize}\,m
\State $\mathbf{o}\gets$ nearest UAV (horizontal distance $d$)
\If{infiltrating \textbf{and} $d<\gv{kEvadeRadius}$\,m} \State $t_{\mathrm{evade}}\gets\gv{kEvadeTime}$\,s \Comment{a UAV is overhead: hide}
\Else \State $t_{\mathrm{evade}}\gets\max(0,t_{\mathrm{evade}}-\Delta t)$ \EndIf
\If{via point reached} \State goal $\gets$ next via point, else the objective \EndIf
\If{time since last plan $\geq$ (infiltrating and not evading ? \gv{kInfiltrateReplan} : \gv{kReplanPeriod})\,s} \State \Call{Replan}{$\mathbf{o}$} \EndIf
\State \Call{FollowLine}{$\Delta t$}
\State hidden $\gets$ all 5 body points blocked from $\mathbf{o}$ (rays against the occluders)
\If{just entered cover} \State maybe pause 1.5--4\,s (personality) \EndIf
\Statex
\Procedure{Replan}{$\mathbf{o}$}
  \If{infiltrating and not evading}
    \State exposed$(c)\gets$ some UAV sees cell $c$
    \State $D\gets$ Dijkstra from the goal cell, step cost $=$ cell $+$ exposure$\cdot[\text{exposed}]$ $+$ noise$(c)$
  \Else
    \State hidden$(c)\gets$ a robot parked on $c$ is invisible from $\mathbf{o}$ (5 test points)
    \State goals $\gets$ hidden cells whose 4 neighbours are hidden too (else any hidden cell)
    \State $D\gets$ breadth-first flood fill from the goals: $D(\text{goal})=0$, $D(c)=1+\min_{n\in N_4(c)}D(n)$
  \EndIf
  \State path $\gets$ from the rover's cell repeatedly step to the neighbour with the smallest $D$ (ties: keep heading)
  \State line $\gets$ \Call{Chaikin}{$[\mathbf{p},\ \mathbf{p}+\mathbf{f}\,(\gv{kSensorForward}+0.1),\ $path$]$, 3}
\EndProcedure
\Procedure{FollowLine}{$\Delta t$}
  \For{$i=0..4$} \State $x_i\gets-0.12+\gv{kSensorSpacing}\,i$; \ $s_i\gets$ dist(sensor $i$, line) $<\gv{kLineHalfWidth}$ \EndFor
  \If{any $s_i$} \State $e\gets\mathrm{mean}\{x_i: s_i\}/\gv{errScale}$; \ $v\gets\gv{kMaxSpeed}\,(1-\gv{slowDown}|e|)$
  \Else \State $e\gets\pm1$ towards the line's side; \ $v\gets$ creep; re-plan if lost for long \EndIf
  \State $\omega\gets\mathrm{clamp}(\gv{kKp}\,e+\gv{kKd}\,\dot e,\ \pm\gv{omegaMax})$; \ wheels $v_{L,R}\gets v\mp\omega\,w/2$ (rate limited)
  \State move along the heading unless the next position collides (then pivot, then back off)
\EndProcedure
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{The motion over the whole base}
\label{sec:motion}
Three motions run at once and react to each other.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\ab{601}{UAV} patrol.} Each \ab{602}{UAV} flies a breathing orbit round the bunker, the two half a turn apart, and its camera
sweeps a ground point ahead of it:
\begin{equation}
\vect g_i(t)=r_i(t)\,\big(\cos\theta_i(t),\ \sin\theta_i(t)\big),\qquad
r_i(t)=r_0+A\sin(\omega_r t+\varphi_i),\qquad \theta_i(t)=\omega_\theta t+\varphi_i,\qquad \varphi_2=\varphi_1+\pi
\label{eq:orbit}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Rover route.} Every re-plan the rover solves Eq.~\eqref{eq:paths} again with the \ab{603}{UAV} positions of that
moment, so the route changes while it is driven; a \ab{604}{UAV} overhead makes the rover stop in cover.
\item \textbf{Strike.} A confirmed track pulls the \ab{605}{UAVs} off the orbit. One intruder gets both \ab{606}{UAVs} (combined strike);
a second intruder that is far away (more than 14\,m from the first) and close to a protected point
takes the nearer \ab{607}{UAV}, and each \ab{608}{UAV} then strikes its own target alone (Section~\ref{sec:game}).
\end{itemize}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\caption{Differences between the path methods. Short forms: BFS: breadth-first search; PD: proportional--derivative.}
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lXXl@{}}
\toprule
Method & Result & Cost & Used by\\\midrule
Flood fill (BFS) & distance to the nearest goal from every cell; any start descends greedily & $O(\text{cells})$ per replan, then $O(1)$ per step & rovers\\
A* & one shortest path, start to goal & expands far fewer cells with a good heuristic & soldiers\\
PD line following & smooth tracking of a drawn line with sensor feedback & 5 samples per tick & rovers\\
Catmull--Rom (centripetal) & smooth curve through the waypoints, no loops or cusps & a few evaluations per sample & soldiers\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Limitation.} The rover's sensors read a line the planner drew, not a line painted in the scene, so the
line-follower layer adds realistic lag and overshoot rather than new information. On top of it a deliberate sideways
weave, a wandering speed and short pauses keep the \ab{609}{UAVs}' tracker from extrapolating a rover along one straight,
constant-speed line.
