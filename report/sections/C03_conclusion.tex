\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ThermalSense takes a small set of unit primitives through every stage of the graphics pipeline and makes each stage
visible inside the running program.



A showcase film of 3\,min\,38\,s (1920$\times$1080, 30\,\ab{624}{fps}, EPIC) is rendered offline by the program: the pipeline
stages and render modes, the vehicles a soldier boards and fires, and a night wave with \ab{625}{UAV} tracking and single and combined strikes. What is missing is listed in
Section~\ref{sec:limits}.
