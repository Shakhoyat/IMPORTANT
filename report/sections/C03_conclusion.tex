\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ThermalSense takes a small set of unit primitives through every stage of the graphics pipeline and makes each stage
visible inside the running program.



A 2-minute showcase video (1920$\times$1080, 60\,\ab{607}{fps}, EPIC) is rendered offline by the program: night wave, \ab{608}{UAV}
tracking and strikes, first-person views, the ray-tracing modes and the day cycle. What is missing is listed in
Section~\ref{sec:limits}.
