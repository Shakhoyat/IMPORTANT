\section{Game-mode AI}
\label{sec:game}
\textit{Builds on: ray casting (\ref{sec:rt}), the thermal pipeline (\ref{sec:thermal}), paths (\ref{sec:paths}).}

At night intruder rovers attack from the perimeter in waves; two UAVs patrol, detect them in their thermal images,
hold a lock for 1.5\,s and launch guided rounds. The player can join in by taking control of a soldier, a tank, the AA
gun or a launcher. Hits are ray tests: each tick a projectile's path segment (previous to current position) is
intersected with every target's bounding sphere and with the static solids' boxes, so a fast round cannot tunnel
through a target between two ticks; an explosion then damages targets inside its blast radius.

\critique{The brief asked for shifts every 6 in-game hours and an explicit ``backup fire'' call from the nearest soldiers.
The code has two shifts (day 06:24--17:36 and night) with staggered changes, and AI soldiers do not fire: ground
backup fire exists only when the player controls a soldier or a weapon. Both UAVs can independently detect, lock
and fire, and they coordinate a combined strike when both hold the same track.}
