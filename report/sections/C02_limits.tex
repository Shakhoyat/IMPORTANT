\section{Limitations and future work}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Primitives:} no level of detail for primitives (Section~\ref{sec:curved}); the ray tracer stands in a
sphere, cylinder or box for seven of the sixteen and leaves the helix out (Section~\ref{sec:prims}).
\item \textbf{Teaching views not built:} interactive clip window, vertex/face normal lines, a live back-face toggle and a
second camera's frustum drawn in the live scene (the Frame Explorer shows the latter two on a frozen frame).
\item \textbf{Thermal panel:} the stages exist (lab figures, Frame Explorer image lab), but there is no single live
tile strip with threshold and equalisation sliders; histogram equalisation and the optional DFT restoration are not in the program.
\item \textbf{Moon:} a fixed-direction night light, not an orbiting body.
\item \textbf{Modes and keys:} no dedicated observation/game switch: enemies follow the time of day, or \key{Shift+F2}.
\item \textbf{AI:} two shifts instead of 6-hour shifts; AI soldiers do not fire, so ``backup fire'' exists only under player control.
\item \textbf{Terrain:} the far hills are scaled spheres; a heightfield ring with splatted materials was planned but
not built.
\item \textbf{Budgets:} vegetation on the high overview (6.6\,ms against 3\,ms) and the static-camera shimmer target
(2.6--6.6/255 against 1.5) are not met.
\item \textbf{Depth precision:} reversed-Z would remove the z-fighting limit but needs OpenGL 4.5.
\item \textbf{Cost of the world update:} all world matrices are recomputed every tick, static buildings included
(Section~\ref{sec:hier}).
\end{itemize}
