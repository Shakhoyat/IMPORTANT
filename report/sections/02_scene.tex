\textbf{Observation and game.} By day there are no enemies, the UAVs stay docked and the soldiers work
(Figure~\ref{fig:hero}h); most teaching views use this setting. At night intruder rovers attack in waves and the UAVs
patrol, detect, lock and fire (Figure~\ref{fig:hero}e, i, Section~\ref{sec:game}); \key{Shift+F2} allows waves by day.

\textbf{Walking in.} The command bunker and the barracks hall are walkable inside; the guard towers and the watchtower
are manned posts on raised platforms. Figures~\ref{fig:walk-bunker}--\ref{fig:walk-posts} follow a soldier at eye
height (1.7\,m). The other buildings are closed shells (Quonset huts, containers, the radar cabin, the guard booth); the
ammunition bunker's sealed chamber is shown by its cutaway in Section~\ref{sec:decomp}.
