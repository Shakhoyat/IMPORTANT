\textbf{Observation and game.} By day there are no enemies, the uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) stay docked and the soldiers work
(Figure~\ref{fig:hero}h); most teaching views use this setting. At night intruder rovers attack in waves and the UAVs
patrol, detect, lock and fire (Figure~\ref{fig:hero}e, i, Section~\ref{sec:game}); \key{Shift+F2} allows waves by day.

\textbf{Walking in.} Every building with a door can be entered on foot. Figures~\ref{fig:walk-bunker} and
\ref{fig:walk-hall} follow a soldier at eye height (1.7\,m) into the command bunker and the barracks hall.
