\textbf{Observation and game.} The scene is always live. By day there are no enemies, the UAVs stay docked on
their pads and the soldiers work (Figure~\ref{fig:hero}e); this is the observation setting used for most teaching
views. At night intruder rovers attack in waves and the UAVs patrol, detect, lock and fire (Figure~\ref{fig:hero}f,
Section~\ref{sec:game}). \key{Shift+F2} (``day ops'') allows waves by day. A separate key that switches enemies
on and off at any hour, as the brief suggested for \key{G}, is not implemented (\key{G} explodes objects into
primitives, Section~\ref{sec:decomp}).
