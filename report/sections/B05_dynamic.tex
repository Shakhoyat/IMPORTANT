\section{Dynamic and controllable objects}
\label{sec:dynamic}
\here{2}
\nControllables{} units can be controlled (soldiers, two \ab{564}{UAVs}, tanks, helicopter, \ab{565}{AA} gun, radar, launchers, blast
doors, gate). Motion is integrated at a fixed 60\,Hz tick; between ticks the drawn pose is interpolated, so motion is
smooth at any frame rate. Almost every follow behaviour (character speed, cameras, doors) uses one tool: a
critically damped spring, which reaches its target as fast as possible without overshoot and has an exact step for any $\Delta t$.
\begin{equation}
\ddot x=-\omega^2(x-x^\ast)-2\omega\dot x\ \Rightarrow\
x(t)=x^\ast+\big((x_0-x^\ast)+(\dot x_0+\omega(x_0-x^\ast))\,t\big)e^{-\omega t}
\label{eq:spring}
\end{equation}
