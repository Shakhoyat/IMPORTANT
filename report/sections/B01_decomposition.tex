The scene is built from \nDesigns{} designs, each the output of one builder function, placed \nDesignInstances{}
times for \sceneParts{} parts in all (Figure~\ref{fig:atlas}). Every card shows the full model beside the same parts
drawn in their primitive's colour and pulled apart. Pale parts belong to a nested design with its own card; $^\dagger$
marks a part hidden at the start (a muzzle flash, a missile in its tube). The legend counts the design's own parts and
matches its parts table.

In the parts tables, repeats share one row: $\pm a$ is a mirrored pair, $a{+}di$ counts copies $i=0,1,\dots$, $\{a,b\}$
lists values and $a..b$ a range. Positions are in the design's frame (cm); rotations are relative to the parent (degrees).

The gait writes only the joint angles of Figure~\ref{fig:soldier-graph}. The phase advances by distance over stride, so
a stance foot stands still on the ground at any speed. During the stance the ankle moves back relative to the hip;
during the swing it follows a cycloid forward with a sine lift. Two-link inverse kinematics then gives the hip and knee
angles that put the ankle there (thigh $L_1=\gait{thigh}$\,m, shin $L_2=\gait{shin}$\,m):
\begin{gather}
x=x_b+(x_f-x_b)\Big(s-\frac{\sin2\pi s}{2\pi}\Big),\quad y=y_0+h\sin\pi s+r(1-s)^2\qquad\text{(swing, }s\in[0,1],\ r=\text{heel rise})\nonumber\\
\kappa=\pi-\arccos\frac{L_1^2+L_2^2-d^2}{2L_1L_2},\quad
\eta=\operatorname{atan2}(x,-y)+\arccos\frac{L_1^2+d^2-L_2^2}{2L_1d},\quad d=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}
\label{eq:gait}
\end{gather}
with $\kappa$ the knee bend and $\eta$ the hip angle (applied as $R_x(-\eta)$ and $R_x(\kappa)$). Walking uses
\gait{speed}\,m/s, stride \gait{stride}\,m, stance \gait{duty} of the cycle, lift $h=\gait{lift}$\,m and a pelvis bob of
\gait{bob}\,m (\gait{cadence_steps_per_min} steps per minute).

boulders, for imported CC0 meshes from Poly Haven; each card names the mesh and its triangle count. Those meshes are
not primitive-built and appear in no table of this section. The cut branches are never swapped.
