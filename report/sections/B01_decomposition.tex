\section{Object decomposition catalogue}
\label{sec:decomp}
\textit{Builds on: primitives (\ref{sec:prims}), modelling transforms (\ref{sec:trs}), hierarchy (\ref{sec:hier}).}

Every object is listed with its assembled view, its exploded view where the program draws one (\key{G}), and a
parts table generated from \code{--export-docs} that covers every part: parts grouped by primitive and material, their
count, the base colour and material (specular weight $k_s$ and Blinn--Phong shininess $n$) of the group's first part,
and the part kinds in the group. For the UAV, the soldier, the tank and the command bunker a second table lists the
largest parts with $S$, $T$, $R$ and parent; every part kind of the UAV and the soldier is shown as the program renders
it (\code{ts --capture parts:<object>}), and the UAV and the tank are shown being built up in hierarchy order
(\code{ts --capture build:<object>}). The workbook sheet \code{Decomposition} lists every single part of the UAV and a soldier with
$T$, $R$, $S$ and parent (Figure~\ref{fig:wb-decomp}). Table~\ref{tab:objs} gives the totals for all objects.

The gait (\code{scene/Gait.cpp}) writes only the joint angles of Figure~\ref{fig:soldier-graph}. The phase advances
by distance over stride, so a stance foot stands still on the ground at any speed. During the stance the ankle moves back
relative to the hip; during the swing it follows a cycloid forward with a sine lift; two-link inverse kinematics then
gives the hip and knee angles that put the ankle there (thigh $L_1=\gait{thigh}$\,m, shin $L_2=\gait{shin}$\,m):
\begin{gather}
x=x_b+(x_f-x_b)\Big(s-\frac{\sin2\pi s}{2\pi}\Big),\quad y=y_0+h\sin\pi s+r(1-s)^2\qquad\text{(swing, }s\in[0,1],\ r=\text{heel rise})\nonumber\\
\kappa=\pi-\arccos\frac{L_1^2+L_2^2-d^2}{2L_1L_2},\quad
\eta=\operatorname{atan2}(x,-y)+\arccos\frac{L_1^2+d^2-L_2^2}{2L_1d},\quad d=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}
\label{eq:gait}
\end{gather}
with $\kappa$ the knee bend and $\eta$ the hip angle (applied as $R_x(-\eta)$ and $R_x(\kappa)$). Walking uses
\gait{speed}\,m/s, stride \gait{stride}\,m, stance \gait{duty} of the cycle, lift $h=\gait{lift}$\,m and a pelvis bob of
\gait{bob}\,m (\gait{cadence_steps_per_min} steps per minute).
