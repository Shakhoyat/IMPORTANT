\section{Colour interpolation}
\label{sec:color}
\here{9}
Colour is interpolated in four places: across a triangle (barycentric), between shading models (Gouraud vs Phong),
between colour spaces (\ab{561}{sRGB} vs linear), and over time (sky and fog presets over the day). Each has a naive version
that fails visibly.
\begin{equation}
c_{\text{lin}}=\begin{cases}c_s/12.92 & c_s\le0.04045\\ \big(\frac{c_s+0.055}{1.055}\big)^{2.4} & \text{else}\end{cases},\qquad
\operatorname{mix}_{\log}(a,b,t)=\exp\big((1-t)\ln a+t\ln b\big)
\label{eq:srgb}
\end{equation}
\begin{gather}
\operatorname{lerp}(\vect a,\vect b,t)=(1-t)\,\vect a+t\,\vect b,\qquad
\text{palette: } \vect c(t)=\operatorname{lerp}\big(\vect c_k,\vect c_{k+1},\,6t-k\big),\quad k=\lfloor6t\rfloor\label{eq:palette}\\
\text{white-hot: } c=t^{0.9},\qquad t\in[0,1]\nonumber
\end{gather}
\begin{itemize}
\item Eq.~\eqref{eq:srgb} is the exact \ab{562}{sRGB} decoding; the shaders decode base colours with the power 2.2 and encode the
final image with the exact piecewise curve (Eq.~\eqref{eq:gamma}, Figure~\ref{fig:tonecurve}b).
\item The seven palette stops $\vect c_0..\vect c_6$ are constants of \code{thermalPalette} in \code{ShaderSources.cpp}, mirrored
on the \ab{563}{CPU} in \code{ThermalPalette.h}; the barycentric mix of three vertex colours is the same $\operatorname{lerp}$ with three weights (Eq.~\eqref{eq:raster}).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Limitation.} Log-space blending is undefined for a zero channel: every preset must stay strictly positive,
and a preset with a near-zero channel makes the blend linger near black for most of the transition. The final image
is stored in 8 bits per channel, and the only defence against banding in dark gradients is the film grain added in
the last pass.
