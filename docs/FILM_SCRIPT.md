### 22:43  From a heat picture to a target

| Time | Picture | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 22:43 | plate `cvsteps`<br>*Five steps, every frame* | Every frame of that picture goes through the same five steps. One: a threshold. Keep only pixels above eighty per cent heat, or above the level that best splits the picture into dark and bright, whichever is higher. Two: remove single stray pixels. Three: join neighbouring hot pixels into regions. Four: measure each region. Five: decide what it is. |
| 23:08 | plate `classify`<br>*Target, person or clutter* | Measuring a region gives its area, its centre, its hottest pixel and how stretched it is. Then the rule. Small, with a very hot core, not too stretched: a vehicle, a target. Warm, without a hot core, upright or compact: a person, and a person is never fired at. Anything else is clutter. |
| 23:29 | picture<br>*Soldiers are marked as persons* | By day you can check it. Through the drone's camera, the soldiers are boxed as persons. None of them is ever a target. |

### 23:37  Tracking

| Time | Picture | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 23:37 | plate `kalman`<br>*Predict, then correct* | One detection is not enough: the camera shakes, pixels flicker, and a rover drives behind a tree. So each target becomes a track with a position, a speed and an uncertainty. Between frames the track is moved ahead by its speed, and the uncertainty grows. A new detection pulls it back, and the uncertainty shrinks. This is a Kalman filter. |
| 24:01 | plate `assoc`<br>*Gating, matching, two views* | Which detection belongs to which track? Only detections inside a track's gate may match, and the pairs are chosen so that the total distance is smallest. When both drones see the same target, their two lines of sight are crossed, which fixes the position much better than one camera can. |
| 24:21 | program: `n_flir`<br>*Tracks coast behind cover* | In the drone's view you can watch it. The boxes are tracks. When the rover goes behind cover, the track coasts on its prediction, and is picked up again when the rover comes out. |
| 24:33 | plate `tracklog`<br>*One track's uncertainty, measured* | Here is that same night as numbers: the uncertainty of one track over time. It jumps while the rover is hidden and falls back each time a camera sees it again. |
