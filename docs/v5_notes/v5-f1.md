## Discrepancies / findings (code vs comments, or behaviour worth a look)
1. MultiTracker merge step (MultiTracker.cpp ~l.260-275): when a confirmed track misses the gate (about 1 % of frames at 99 % gating) a newborn tentative track appears at the measurement, and the merge copies the NEWBORN's state over the older confirmed track (`std::copy(drop.x...)`, `drop.P`): velocity jumps to 0 with sigma 2 m/s (see figdata kalman search: 53 consecutive seeds of a 14 s straight/turn/straight run all had at least one such reset). The comment says "keep the older id with the fresher state"; for a CV filter it would be better to keep the old state (or only adopt the position). The Kalman figure uses a seed (4295) without such an event.
2. Single-ray ground hits are biased: Squad turns a blob centroid into a ray and intersects the TERRAIN (imagePointToGround), but the blob is about 0.2 m above the ground, so the fix is pushed about 0.27 m beyond the rover at 37 deg depression (visible as a constant -0.3 m offset in F7). The ray march uses 5 cm steps (quantisation stripes in the scatter). Two-view fusion removes the bias.
3. HideController.h header: "the 20 m disc is divided into square cells": the grid is 80 x 80 cells of 0.5 m = 40 m square, free radius 18.3 m (kFreeRadius), comment of kFreeRadius says "berm / perimeter fence (r = 19 m)" while kBermRadius is 17.4 m.
4. CvPipeline.h comment says t = max(fixed floor, Otsu); the code uses max(floor, Otsu + 1) (pixels > Otsu), cosmetic.
5. ShaderSources.cpp: the lamp glints on wet terrain (terrainShade, ~l.555) multiply the practical colour by 3.0 while compose() does not, so a lamp is 3x brighter in a puddle than on a wet metal part at the same roughness. SSR default budget is 48 steps (Renderer.h), 64 only on one quality level.
6. Drive trace: a rover with seed 11 spends 756 of 2100 frames evading (UAV within 5 m) and had not reached the objective after 70 s; the guide line is replanned at every mode change so the line shown at t = 0 is not the line followed later.
7. CombinedStrike event `value` is |T_lead - T_wing| in ms (flight-time difference), not the planned arrival spread as the GameEvents.h/Squad comments suggest (arrival spread is 0 by construction); events pushed by Squad have time = 0 (only Scene::pushEvent stamps them), so a consumer cannot time-order them.
